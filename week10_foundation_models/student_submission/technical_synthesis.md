From all three missions, turning a model's response into reliable robot behavior requires balancing a clear prompt with physical constraints. In mission 1, capability cards helped limit the LLM to valid movements like navigating and inspecting, even though the small model still got confused about starting states.  In Mission 2, setting prompts in visual evidence helped the VLM look for the right tools, but it still struggled with accurate object classification.  In Mission 3, direct prompts about the environment reduced hesitation compared to vague goals, but the robot still got stuck in looping behaviors when it hit walls. One major remaining limitation is the gap between high-level reasoning and physical execution; small models easily lose track of multi-step coordinates, showing that foundation models must be paired with safety rules.
